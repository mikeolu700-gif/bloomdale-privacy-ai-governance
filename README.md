## Portfolio disclosure

All case studies use **Meridian Digital Holdings**, a fictional enterprise, and synthetic records, vendors, systems, risk findings, and assessment data. They are independent professional demonstrations, not real client engagements, legal advice, or evidence of actual production compliance. Fictional release decisions such as **HOLD** are deliberate governance outcomes within the scenarios.
