About the Portfolio

This portfolio is independently developed under BloomDale Capital LLC, an investment holding and business consulting company.

Led by Olumuyiwa Sikiru Michael, a data privacy and governance professional with over 15 years of experience across technology, data management, privacy operations, and regulatory compliance.

The portfolio demonstrates how privacy and governance professionals identify regulatory obligations, assess risk, design controls, and communicate recommendations to business leadership.
